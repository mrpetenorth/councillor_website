My Plan for Bricknell

Environment

Looking after and improving our precious green spaces such as Cropton Park, Jack Kaye Field and Bakers Field, and making our part of Hull brighter and greener.

Improving Road Safety

Building better public transport links, making roads safer for cars, cyclists and pedestrians, and encouraging safe and responsible parking.

Building our Community

Supporting community events, helping residents come together, and growing a stronger sense of togetherness across Bricknell.
